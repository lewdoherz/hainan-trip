# Your one-big-suitcase packing strategy

With your luggage limits, I would use the **large checked suitcase primarily for clothing, diapers, swim gear and toiletries**. Diapers take space but weigh almost nothing; compression bags work very well for them.

Your **two carry-ons should contain enough family clothing for 24 hours, medicine and critical baby supplies**. Your two backpacks are the things you actually need while sitting on the airplane.

One useful trick: divide the children's diapers between luggage. For example, don't put all 70 baby diapers in the checked suitcase. Put **10 baby + 8 toddler diapers in cabin baggage** so a delayed suitcase isn't immediately a crisis.
